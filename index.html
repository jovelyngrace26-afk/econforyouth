/ 02 — About Us

Economics,
minus the jargon.

EconForYouth started with a simple question: Is there an explanation behind everything around us?

From everyday decisions to global events, economics shapes more of our world than we often realize. EconForYouth explores these connections through relatable insights, discussions, and perspectives that make economics easier to understand and closer to everyday life. Join us as we learn, question, and discover the economic stories behind the world around us.

— Founded by Jovelyn Grace
