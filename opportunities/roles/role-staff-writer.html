Staff Writer

2–3 hrs / week Remote Rolling applications

About the role

Staff Writers research and produce well-informed articles on economic concepts, current issues, and real-world applications for EconForYouth. The role focuses on making economics clear, relevant, and accessible to a student audience.

What you'll do

  • Research and develop relevant article topics.
  • Contribute consistently to the publication.
  • Use credible sources and provide proper citations in APA format.
  • Pitch and write 1–2 articles per month on a topic of your choice.

What we're looking for

  • Strong writing and research skills.
  • Genuine interest in economics, business, and current affairs.
  • Reliability, attention to detail, and willingness to learn.

Why join

Join EconForYouth to develop your writing and research skills, contribute to meaningful economic discussions, and have your work published as part of a growing student-led publication.
