Content Researcher

2-3 posts / week Rolling applications

About the role

Content Researchers find interesting, relevant, and timely economics topics for EconForYouth. They suggest and research approved topics using reliable sources, then provide key facts and examples for Instagram posts. Researchers will work with Graphic Designers to turn their research into engaging content.

What you'll do

  • Research 2–3 posts per week
  • Suggest topics based on current events, trends, and youth interests
  • Gather useful facts, statistics, and real-world examples
  • Coordinate with Graphic Designers on the final content

What we're looking for

  • Ability to recognize interesting and relevant topics
  • Curiosity about economics and current events
  • Strong research and critical-thinking skills

Why join

Build research and analytical skills, contribute to published content, and have your work reach a growing youth audience.
