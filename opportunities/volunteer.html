/ Volunteer

Help us make economics make sense to more young people.

Econ For Youth is a student-led nonprofit run entirely by students. Volunteer with Econ For Youth as a writer, researcher, designer, or organizer — remote, flexible, and open to students everywhere. No prior experience required, just curiosity.

/ Why join us
01

Build real experience

Work with a small team on real deadlines, real editing, and real feedback.

02

Learn by teaching

Explaining economics to teens is one of the fastest ways to actually understand it.

03

Flexible & remote

Contribute a couple hours a week, on your own schedule, from anywhere.

04

Get published

See your name on articles that reach students well beyond your own school.

05

Meet driven peers

Join a small community of curious, ambitious students who care about the same things you do.
